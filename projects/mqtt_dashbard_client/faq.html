Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the Premium subscription and app features

Note: In your region the Premium subscription works through an ad-supported model. Enable ads to get all Premium features for free.

Premium Subscription

How do I get access to Premium features?

Open the Premium section in the app and enable the Enable Premium toggle. Ads will be shown in the app, and all Premium features will be unlocked for free.

How does the ad-supported Premium work?

Payment is not available in your region. Instead, you can get Premium for free by enabling ads:

  1. Open the Premium section in the app
  2. Enable the Enable Premium toggle
  3. Ads will be shown in the app
  4. All Premium features become available immediately
How do I turn off the ad-supported Premium?

Open the Premium section in the app and disable the Enable Premium toggle. Ad display stops together with the Premium features.

You can enable it again at any time the same way.

App Features

How do I transfer my settings to a new device?

There are two ways:

  • Backup (file): Settings → Backup configuration — create a file, transfer it to the new device and use Restore configuration. Best for moving to a specific device.
  • Share configurations over MQTT: Settings → Share configurations over MQTT — export the configuration on one device, enable receiving on the other and import it. Best for keeping several devices in sync.

Learn more in the Share configurations over MQTT section of the documentation.

Can I add multiple addresses for one broker?

Yes. In the broker editor tap Add endpoint to add a backup address. If the primary address is unavailable, the app automatically connects to the next one.

Learn more in the Broker Endpoints section of the documentation.
